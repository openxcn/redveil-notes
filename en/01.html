Red Veil Racing Is in Public Beta: Free Entry, and the Winner Is Decided by a Block Hash That Doesn't Exist Yet

Published by the RedMagic NFT team. Not investment advice.

Published by the official RedMagic NFT team. Live site https://redveil.redmagic.pro. It currently runs in public beta on BNB Smart Chain mainnet, chainId 56.

Three things up front.

Entry is free, and you do not need to hold a RedMagic NFT to race. The result is not decided by the host and not decided by us — it is computed from a block hash produced after the race starts, combined with a snapshot of every horse's stats taken at the starting line. And this is a beta: the contracts still carry admin rights, and the tokens are real on-chain assets. Check addresses and amounts yourself before you touch anything.

Getting a horse into a race

A horse is an NFT on chain (contract 0x70a3B7E937DCA935Be59A98f35036325beeb5329). Its stats are sex, gene value, training, level, wins, pregnancy and cooldown state, lifespan, and boarding status.

There is exactly one prerequisite for entering: the horse must be boarded at a stable hall. Boarding is paid per day, directly to the stable owner, non-refundable, minimum 30 days per purchase. A boarded horse is locked — it cannot be listed for sale, but it can race. For the days it is racing, the race host covers the boarding fee, and the owner's own boarding period is extended by the same number of days.

The first genesis blind box run — 420 boxes — is fully sold out. It produced 210 horses: 10 male, 200 female. Today the most direct way to get a horse is to buy one on the marketplace, or to redeem a genesis card you already hold via "My Genesis Cards".

How a race actually starts

Hosts post collateral to open a race: 100,000 R-MAB minimum for an R-MAB race, 50 USDT for a USDT race. Collateral can be rolled over indefinitely.

At six horses, the host may start the race manually. If they don't, entries stay open until twelve. Once twelve horses are in, a betting window opens (currently set to 30 minutes on-chain), and the host can start early at any time. When the window expires, betting closes and the race starts; the on-chain start buffer is currently 0, anyone can trigger it, and the system also starts it automatically, so a host who goes offline cannot stall the race.

At the moment the race starts, every horse's stats are snapshotted. Changing stats afterwards has no effect on the result.

Where the money goes

Place prizes are 5,000 / 3,000 / 2,000 in an R-MAB race (5 / 3 / 2 USDT in a USDT race), paid out of the host's collateral and scaled down proportionally if the collateral is short.

Bets are fixed size: 1,000 R-MAB, or 1 USDT. The betting pool splits 100% into three parts — 75% to backers of the winning horse, proportional to stake; 5% as a rebate to horse owners, by volume on each horse; 20% to the platform's four-tier split.

That 20% divides as treasury 30%, proposer 30%, inviter 30%, foundation senior 10%.

A wallet's first bet has a 3× floor including stake (a top-up of at most 2×, first bet only, no stacking). The top-up comes from the host's collateral, capped at 20% of collateral per race.

The claim window is 24 hours. Unclaimed payouts revert to the host as sediment, withdrawable under "My → Earnings".

The risk rule is a condition, not a slogan

If three or more wallets back the winning horse, and either 80%+ of them placed exactly one bet in the entire race and it was on that horse, or every betting wallet in the race backed the same horse, the race is flagged: all funds in that race move to the foundation treasury, the host is blacklisted, and the site pauses.

Getting started

Open https://redveil.redmagic.pro, connect a wallet, confirm the network is BSC mainnet (chainId 56), board a horse under "Racing → Stable Hall", then enter a race under "Races" and wait for six horses.

The full rules live in-game under "My → About → Official Docs" — twelve sections at https://redveil.redmagic.pro/#docs. If a number in the docs disagrees with what the page shows, the page and the chain win.
