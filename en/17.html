Red Veil Racing is a blockchain horse-racing game on BNB Smart Chain. Most explainers talk about horses. The more interesting part is that the game has three different roles, and each one is paid from a different slice of the same betting pool.

The three roles

  • Club owner: opens a stable, hosts races, collects boarding fees.
  • Horse owner: keeps and races horses. Boarding is paid per day in R-MAB to the stable owner, prepaid and non-refundable, with a minimum of 30 days per payment.
  • Spectator: only bets on the winner.

How the pool is split

Every bet goes into one pool, and the pool is divided 100% into three parts:

  1. 75% champion payout — split pro-rata among everyone who backed the winner. If nobody backed the winner, this 75% goes to the club owner at settlement.
  2. 5% horse owner rebate — paid to the owner of each horse in proportion to the bets placed on that horse.
  3. 20% platform four-tier split — vault 30% / proposer 30% / referrer 30% / foundation club owner 10%.

That is the whole economics of a single race. There is no hidden fourth slice: 75 + 5 + 20 = 100.

Why the roles matter

Because the three payouts come from the same pool, "who wins" is not the only question. A horse owner earns from the 5% rebate whether or not their horse comes first. A club owner earns boarding fees from every stabled horse, and takes the full 75% when nobody backed the winner. A spectator's result depends only on the 75%.

How the winner is decided

Results are not drawn by a server. The finishing order is derived from a future block hash plus a snapshot of horse stats taken when the race starts, so the outcome cannot be pre-computed before the race closes. The rules are published as twelve in-game documentation sections that can be checked line by line.

Facts worth checking yourself

  • Minimum 6 horses to start; entry stays open up to 12 horses, then a betting window of at most 12 hours.
  • First-season genesis boxes were 420 in total and produced exactly 210 horses (10 male / 200 female).
  • Market trades carry a 20% tax; the seller receives 80%, and the tax goes entirely to the four-tier split.

If any number here disagrees with the on-chain contracts, trust the contracts.
