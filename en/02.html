Twelve Sections, One Page: How Red Veil Writes Its Rules Down

Personal notes, not an official announcement. Not investment advice.

Personal notes, not an official announcement. Live site: https://redveil.redmagic.pro

Where the docs live

Not on the marketing site — inside the game. Open https://redveil.redmagic.pro, go to "My → About → Official Docs", or jump straight to https://redveil.redmagic.pro/#docs.

That placement is deliberate. The documentation sits on the same page as the contracts, buttons and numbers it describes, so when a section tells you a fee, you can switch tabs and check it against the interface.

One honest caveat: www.redmagic.pro/docs currently hosts four other documentation sets — AI Crypto Wallet, RedMagicBox, AGI, and RedMagic equity NFT — 47 pages in total. The twelve racing sections are not on that site and are not in its sitemap. If you search the corporate site for racing rules and find nothing, the docs are not missing; they are in-game.

What each section covers

01 Quick start — three steps, and what the five tabs (Races, Racing, Market, Blind Box, My) each do.

02 Core concepts — a horse is an NFT; boarding and locking; two billing paths (prepaid balance vs. direct wallet payment); four roles (regular player, member, host, foundation senior).

03 Raising a horse — training (free horses in the training camp, boarded horses at the stable), boarding (daily prepayment, non-refundable, 30-day minimum, extended during races), feeding and items, breeding (80% of the stud fee to the stallion's owner, 20% through the four-tier split; 12-hour stallion cooldown; 3-day post-foaling cooldown; 5 pregnancies max), the offspring gene formula, foal care, lifespan, auto-entry.

04 Races — who may host, collateral thresholds, entry conditions, starting and closing, where results come from, place prizes, betting and payout, claims and sediment, the risk rule, the host console, custom-token races, naming and sharing, venue subpages.

05 Market — only free horses may be listed; 20% tax on sale, seller nets 80%.

06 Blind boxes — the first 420-box run is fully opened; a second run is unscheduled; genesis cards already issued never expire, are transferable, and can be redeemed under "My Genesis Cards".

07 Identity and revenue — one RedMagic NFT ID, bound for life, non-transferable. No NFT is needed to enter, bet, breed, or list a stud; it is needed to host. Referral links take the form #venue/host-address?ref=id; a ?ref on the homepage is not currently recorded.

08 Assets and withdrawal — balances, prepaid funds, sediment, withdrawals; keep BNB for gas.

09 Network and contracts — BSC mainnet, chainId 56, RPC bsc-dataseed1.bnbchain.org, plus fourteen contract addresses, each linking to the explorer.

10 FAQ — fifteen entries, including the ones people actually ask: why the entry button is greyed out; whether boarding fees are refundable.

11 UI message reference — every prompt the interface can show, mapped to its cause.

12 Risk and disclosure — negative-sum structure, admin rights during beta, and token volatility, written into the docs themselves rather than only into marketing copy.

Three reading paths

If you just want to play: 01 → 02 → 04 → 10.

If you want to host: all of 04, especially collateral, sediment, the risk rule, and "cover entrants' boarding".

If you want to check the code: take the addresses from 09, then read 03 and 04 for the ratios, and compare each one on BscScan.

One self-imposed rule

The last line of the docs says: if a number here disagrees with the live page, the page and the chain take precedence. During beta the parameters move. Stating the precedence order up front is cheaper than explaining a discrepancy later.
