Where the 3x First-Bet Subsidy Actually Comes From

Published by the RedMagic NFT team. Not investment advice.

The "3x subsidy" on a wallet's first bet is not a platform giveaway, and it is not a profit promise. It is paid out of the organizer's deposit, with a hard budget cap. Understanding that changes how you read it.

Who funds it

An organizer must post a deposit before a race can start. Each race has a subsidy budget capped at 20% of that deposit. Once the budget is used, it is gone — there is no top-up loop.

Who receives it

  • Only the first bet from a single wallet counts
  • The multiple is 3x including principal, meaning at most 2x is added
  • Additional bets from the same wallet do not stack

That "first bet only" rule is the important part. It limits the subsidy to a first-try action instead of creating an arbitrage loop for repeat bettors.

What happens if the deposit is short

The organizer has to top it up. R-MAB races and USDT races each have their own minimum, calculated separately — consistent with the dual-currency design where the two pools never mix.

Do not forget boarding

A horse must be boarded at an organizer's stable to race. Boarding is paid daily in R-MAB to the stable owner, is non-refundable, has a 30-day minimum, and can be prepaid for longer with no upper limit. That is a cost, not a deposit.

So the full cost structure has three layers: boarding (daily, prepaid, non-refundable) + deposit (minimums, may need topping up) + bet principal (can be lost entirely).

Three common misreadings

  1. The 3x subsidy has a cap — it is not unlimited downside protection
  2. It guarantees a payout multiple on the first bet, not that your horse wins and certainly not that you profit
  3. The deposit is the organizer's obligation, not a platform guarantee
