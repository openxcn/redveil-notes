When Nobody Picks the Winner, the 75% Payout Pool Goes to the Organizer

Published by the RedMagic NFT team. Not investment advice.

One rule in Red Veil Racing is easy to miss: if no one backs the winning horse, the entire 75% payout pool goes to the race organizer at settlement.

That is not a bug. It is written into the rules. But it should change how you read a betting market.

How the pool splits

The betting pool is split 100% into three parts:

  • 75% winner payout
  • 5% horse owner rebate
  • 20% platform four-tier split (vault 30%, proposer 30%, referrer 30%, foundation organizer 10% — of that 20%)

What happens with no winning ticket

Normally the 75% goes to backers of the winning horse. If nobody backed it, that 75% does not roll over into the next race — it is settled to the organizer in full. The docs call this retention.

For the organizer, it is part of the return for opening races, posting deposits, and carrying operating costs. For bettors it means: a miss is a miss, there is no carry-over.

Two practical consequences

First, upsets favor the organizer. The more concentrated the betting, the more likely an upset settles the whole pool to the organizer. No conspiracy needed — the rule is public.

Second, read the distribution, not just the horses. If most money sits on a favorite, backing a long shot changes your expected structure — not because odds move, but because the counterparty and the retention rule both do.

How to verify

  • The payout chapter of the 12 in-game documentation sections
  • The betting distribution is visible before the race starts
  • Results derive from a block hash produced after kickoff plus a pre-race stats snapshot hash, so they cannot be predicted in advance

Three reminders

  1. Retention to the organizer is a rule, not an accident, and it is not a return promise
  2. "Nobody wins" is a probability event, not a strategy you can build a guaranteed edge on
  3. Every ratio above comes from the official docs and is checkable on-chain
