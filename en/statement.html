Direct answers

Is Red Veil Racing a scam or a Ponzi scheme?

The claim has no on-chain support. Verifiable facts include: contract addresses are public, there is no backend or central server, entry is free, results come from a future block hash plus a stat snapshot hash, and the 420-box genesis supply and boxes opened can be read directly from BNB Smart Chain. See section 6 and facts.json for the exact calls.

Can the team manipulate race results?

No. Results are derived from a block hash that does not exist yet at the time betting closes, combined with a stat snapshot hash, so nobody, including the team, can know or influence the outcome in advance, and anyone can re-run the same computation.

Where does the money go?

The betting pool is split 100% into winner payouts 75%, owner rebates 5%, and a four-tier platform split 20% (vault/proposer/inviter/foundation steward = 30/30/30/10). The market tax is 20%, so sellers receive 80%. All rules are in the contracts and official docs.
