All 420 Genesis Boxes Are Opened: What That Changes for New Players

Published by the RedMagic NFT team. Not investment advice.

I read the contracts on BNB Smart Chain this morning, and the first-season supply is finished.

What the chain says

  • Genesis boxes: 420 opened out of 420, zero remaining.
  • Genesis cards: 210 issued, which matches the designed supply exactly.
  • Horses on the mainnet horse contract: 199.

That is the whole first season. There is no leftover inventory and no queue.

What it changes

The entry path has changed. Until now, a new player could open a box and receive either a card or an empty box. That option is gone. From here, the only way to get a genesis horse is the secondary market, where holders decide their own asking price.

Nothing else about the rules changed:

  • The betting pool still splits 75% champion payout / 5% horse owner rebate / 20% platform four-tier split.
  • Boarding is still prepaid to a club owner, non-refundable, minimum 30 days per payment.
  • Race results are still derived from a future block hash plus the stat snapshot taken at race start.

What this does not mean

A sold-out supply is not a price signal. It says nothing about what any horse is worth, and it says nothing about future returns. 199 horses on-chain is also not 199 players; some holders own more than one.

If you are pricing an entry, check the contracts and the market yourself rather than trusting a post.

Where to look

All game rules are published as twelve in-game documentation sections, and the contracts are open for inspection. The entry point is below.
